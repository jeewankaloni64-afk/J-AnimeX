Copyright & DMCA Policy

Last Updated: August 20, 2026

1. Copyright Respect

J-AnimeX respects the intellectual property rights of copyright owners. We expect users and contributors to respect applicable copyright laws.

If you believe that material available through J-AnimeX infringes your copyright, you may contact us with the information described below.

2. Copyright Complaint

A copyright complaint should contain enough information for us to understand and review the claim. Where applicable, please provide:

  • Your full name and contact information.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work you believe has been infringed.
  • The URL or other location of the material you are referring to.
  • An explanation of why you believe the material infringes your copyright.
  • A statement that the information provided is accurate to the best of your knowledge.
  • Any additional information reasonably necessary to review the complaint.

3. How to Contact Us

Please send copyright-related requests through the contact information provided on our Contact Us page.

Contact: Contact Us

4. Review of Complaints

After receiving a legitimate copyright complaint, we may review the information provided and take appropriate action where required or appropriate under applicable law.

Possible actions may include restricting access to the identified material or requesting additional information from the parties involved.

5. False or Misleading Claims

Copyright complaints should be submitted only when the sender has a genuine basis for making the claim. False, fraudulent or misleading claims may have legal consequences.

6. Counter-Notification

Where applicable under the law governing a particular complaint, a person affected by a copyright removal request may have rights to respond or submit a counter-notification.

Any such process will depend on the applicable law and circumstances of the complaint.

7. Policy Updates

J-AnimeX may update this Copyright & DMCA Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page.

8. Important Notice

This page provides general information and is not legal advice. The appropriate copyright procedure may depend on the country, applicable law and circumstances involved.
